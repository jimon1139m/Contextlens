## Detailed Description (Max 16,000 chars)
**ContextLens** is the ultimate companion for power users of AI chat platforms like Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and DeepSeek.

Have you ever hit a token limit? Lost track of conversation context? ContextLens works seamlessly in the background to automatically compress your prompts and intelligently manage your chat history, ensuring you get the most out of every single message.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Heuristic Prompt Compression:** Automatically strips unnecessary conversational fluff while preserving critical technical details, saving you tokens without sacrificing quality.
- **Local RAG Pipeline:** Upload your own Markdown or Text files to the extension's local Knowledge Base. ContextLens automatically retrieves the most relevant chunks of information and prepends them to your prompts behind the scenes.
- **History Trimming:** Keep your conversations running infinitely. ContextLens intelligently truncates older messages to keep you within the context budget.
- **100% Privacy Focused:** All vector embeddings and data processing happen **locally on your machine** using `@xenova/transformers`. No data is ever sent to external servers.
